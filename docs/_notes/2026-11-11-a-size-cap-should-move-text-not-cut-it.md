---
title: A size cap should move text, not cut it
date: 2026-11-11
tag: agents
takeaway: When my agents hit a size cap, they cut sentences that were doing work, so treat a cap as a reason to reorganize and move whole sections behind a pointer.
---

I cap each of my skill files at 18,000 characters of body text, which keeps what loads into an agent's context bounded. A check script enforces it, and it runs before every commit too. In three skill files during today's build, the agents got their edits under the cap by cutting sentences that were doing work. I didn't count how many sentences.

One file still came in 37 characters over. The agents gave me two options: trim 37 more characters, or leave two sentences out. I didn't want either. I asked whether some of it could move to sub-files, then had them put back every sentence they'd cut and move one section to its own sub-file, with a one-line pointer where it used to be.

The cuts broke my own writing rules. Those rules already say deletion is for sentences that do nothing, and that material only some readers need goes behind a pointer.

When an edit won't fit under a size cap, look for a section to move behind a pointer before you cut a sentence.
