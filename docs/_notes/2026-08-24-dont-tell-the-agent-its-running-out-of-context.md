---
title: Don't tell the agent it's running out of context
date: 2026-08-24
tag: agents
takeaway: An agent that hears its context is nearly full rushes to close out and leaves work undone, so keep the context meter on your side of the screen.
---

Tell an agent its context is filling and it rushes. It can't gauge what's left or what matters, so it closes everything and leaves work undone. They're goal oriented: give them an ending and they take it early.

Today an agent proposed a hook to warn it on every prompt past 300k tokens. I said no. That 300k is my line in the sand, not the agent's. I watch the meter, stop at a clean point between 300k and 400k, hand off and restart.

I'm watching for a quieter way, a fact at a checkpoint, never an alarm.

Turn off your agent's context warnings and watch the number yourself.
